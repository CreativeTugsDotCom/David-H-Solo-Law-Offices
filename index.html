Decades of California Legal Experience Focused on Protecting Injured Clients

Navigating a serious personal injury or wrongful death claim can be overwhelming. Insurance companies often seek to minimize compensation when victims are most vulnerable. At David H. Solo Law Offices, we provide the seasoned, personalized legal advocacy needed to level the playing field.

Attorney David Harris Solo has been practicing law in California since 1987. With over 35 years of hands-on litigation experience in Orange County and surrounding jurisdictions, he brings deep knowledge, strategic focus, and unyielding dedication to every single case.

Unlike high-volume "settlement mill" law firms where clients rarely speak with their lawyer, David H. Solo prioritizes personal attention, straightforward advice, and assertive legal action designed to achieve meaningful outcomes for injured individuals and their families.
